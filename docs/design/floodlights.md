# FutSlot's look: floodlights on

Futsal in the Kathmandu valley is mostly played after work and college, on rented artificial-turf courts under
floodlights, booked by the hour, usually by phoning around. FutSlot's job is to show which hours are free and book
one. So the hour is the hero: a free hour is lit, floodlight yellow, and everything around it stays quiet.

## Colour

| Name | Light | Dark | Use |
|------|-------|------|-----|
| Turf | `#11673A` | `#1E8C50` | Brand: the hero band, primary buttons, links that act |
| Turf deep | `#0B4D2B` | `#0F3D24` | Pressed states, the owners' band |
| Chalk | `#F8FAF6` | `#0C1611` | Page background: a cool white with a breath of green, not cream |
| Floodlight | `#FFD23F` | `#FFD23F` | Free hours only (and the one action on a turf band). Nothing else is yellow |
| Ink | `#14231A` | `#E8EFE9` | Text |
| Line | `#DCE3DB` | `#24342B` | Borders and court lines on light ground |

## Type

- **Khand** (600, 700): headings, prices and hours. A condensed face with Latin and Devanagari in one family, so
  Nepali pages keep the same scoreboard voice. Hours are set big: `7 PM` reads like a scoreboard.
- **Mukta** (400, 500, 700): everything else. A humanist sans made for Devanagari and Latin together.

## Layout

- Left aligned throughout. One turf band at the top of the front page, with the court's half-way line and centre
  circle drawn in chalk; nothing else on the page is green-filled except buttons and the owners' band.
- Venue cards are a photo and words, no boxes, borders or shadows. Each shows the next free hours today as lit
  hour chips that book straight away.
- Free hours are the same lit tiles on the venue page and in the player app. Booked hours aren't shown on public
  pages; in the app they're quiet and struck through.
- How booking works is a real sequence, so it keeps big Khand numerals; nothing else is numbered.

## Rules

- Yellow means "free, book it". Never decoration, never a second meaning.
- No all-caps labels, no eyebrows above headings, no middle-dot meta strings, no arrows in button text.
- Radius 6px on controls and photos, 999px only on chips. No drop shadows.
